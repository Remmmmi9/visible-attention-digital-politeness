Digital Map

This Digital Map records the digital communication spaces I use most often in everyday life.

I want to show that different platforms do not give me the same communication experience. Even when I am simply “sending a message”, my reply speed, tone, pressure and self-presentation can change across WhatsApp, WeChat, Email, Teams and social media.

This page records how these spaces shape my communication habits, and what kind of communicator I become in each space.

01

WhatsApp

Fast, visible, task-based

WhatsApp often feels like a fast-paced space for me, especially in group work, society communication and practical tasks.

The strongest feeling it gives me is visibility. Read receipts, timestamps and short replies all make me aware that other people may know I have seen the message, and may also notice when I reply.

Because of this, even when I am still checking information or thinking about how to say something in English, I often feel that I should respond first. On the surface, WhatsApp is just a chat tool, but in group work or practical communication, it can also carry responsibility and reply pressure.

Reflection

After organising this Digital Map, I became more aware that platforms are not just tools for sending messages.

They affect how I speak, how quickly I reply, how much emotion I show, and how I present myself to others. Different platforms have different rhythms and expectations, and I gradually adjust my communication style within these spaces.

So reply pressure is not only my own emotion. It is also connected to the functions, habits and social expectations of these digital spaces.
