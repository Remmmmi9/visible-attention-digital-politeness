About / The Maker

About this project and the person behind it.

This page introduces the perspective behind the archive: a digital media student moving between languages, platforms and different expectations of reply.

My perspective

I started this project from a feeling I know well: sometimes I have seen a message, but I am not ready to reply yet.

That pause may look small, but for me it can include thinking, checking, translating, softening my tone, or trying not to be misunderstood.

For the audience

This website is for people who often move between different communication spaces: friends, group work, societies, email, social media, and messages in another language.

It may also speak to anyone who has paused before replying because they needed time to think, check, translate, soften their tone, or avoid being misunderstood.

What I hope it offers

I hope this project makes small digital moments easier to notice.

It does not try to give one clear answer. It simply slows down the space before a reply, and looks at what might be happening inside it.

About me

Hi, I’m Simiao.

My work often moves between digital culture, communication, visual storytelling and personal reflection.

This project comes from my own experience of moving between WeChat, WhatsApp, email and group-work platforms as an international student. I do not treat my experience as universal. I use it as a starting point to ask a more public question.

I am interested in the small signs people use to communicate care, hesitation and politeness: a message bubble, a read receipt, an emoji, a short explanation, or a sentence rewritten several times before being sent.
